Per-scene voiceover clips go here (see ../../VOICEOVER.md).

Layout — one MP3 per scene, per language:
  vo/ar/title.mp3   vo/en/title.mp3
  vo/ar/hook.mp3    vo/en/hook.mp3
  vo/ar/concept.mp3 vo/en/concept.mp3
  vo/ar/data.mp3    vo/en/data.mp3
  vo/ar/recap.mp3   vo/en/recap.mp3
  vo/ar/outro.mp3   vo/en/outro.mp3

Each scene auto-lengths to its clip. A missing clip = that scene stays silent at
its default length, so you can add them incrementally. Scripts are in VOICEOVER.md
and src/content.ts (lesson.narration).
